# Battery Standards

Automotive batteries are manufactured to different national and international standards. Each standard defines physical dimensions, terminal configurations, performance test methods, and rating systems.

---

## Standards Overview

| Standard | Full Name | Region | Terminal Style | Hold-Down |
|----------|-----------|--------|---------------|-----------|
| **JIS** | Japanese Industrial Standard | Asia, Africa, Middle East, Oceania | T1 (small/large post) | B00 (top frame) |
| **DIN** | Deutsches Institut fur Normung | Europe, Middle East, Africa | T1 (standard post) | B13 (bottom rail) |
| **BCI** | Battery Council International | North America | SAE (top post/side terminal) | Various |
| **AS** | Australian Standard | Australia | T1 | B00 |
| **SA** | South African Standard | South Africa | T1 | B00 |

!!! note "Regional Overlap"
    Many markets accept multiple standards. For example, JIS batteries are widely used in Africa alongside DIN batteries. The Middle East imports both JIS and DIN specification batteries.
