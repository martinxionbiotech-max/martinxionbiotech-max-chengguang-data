# 55D23 Battery — 75D23

The 55D23 is the mid-compact JIS battery — bridging the narrow 55B24 and the full-size 65D26. It is the standard battery for compact-to-mid Japanese sedans.

## How to Identify This Battery

Standard D-series width (173 mm) with a shorter length (232 mm) than the 65D26 (260 mm). The 28 mm shorter length is the distinguishing feature.

## Vehicle Applications

Compact-to-mid sedans with 1.6L-2.0L engines. Popular for Toyota Corolla and Honda Civic.

### Compatible Vehicles

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 55D23 vs 65D26?

65D26 is 28 mm longer with higher CCA (450-580A vs 500A). Upgrade if tray allows the extra length.

### Is 75D23 the same?

Same case. 75D23 typically indicates higher capacity (75Ah class). Verify the Ah on the label.
