## Frequently Asked Questions

### 190H52 vs 145G51?

190H52 is larger (521x278 vs 508x222 mm), heavier (45-55 vs 30-38 kg), higher capacity (200 vs 135Ah). 190H52 for heavy trucks, 145G51 for medium trucks.

### Is N200 the same?

Yes — N200 is the regional designation for 190H52.

### What about 24V systems?

Use two 190H52 batteries in series for 24V heavy truck/equipment starting systems.
