The 145G51 (N150) is a commercial-grade heavy-duty JIS battery — built for medium-duty trucks, large buses, construction equipment, and marine applications. Thick positive plates (3.0+ mm), lead-antimony grids, and a deep sump design deliver extreme vibration resistance and sustained high-current capability that passenger car batteries cannot match.

## How to Identify This Battery

Massive case (508 x 222 mm). T1 large post terminals (O19.5mm positive, O17.9mm negative). Label shows '145G51' or 'N150'. The weight alone (30-38 kg) is the most obvious identifier — roughly double a passenger car battery.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 145G51 vs 190H52: which do I need?

145G51 for medium trucks and buses (4-8L diesel). 190H52 for heavy trucks and equipment (>8L diesel). 190H52 is larger, heavier, and more powerful.

### Is N150 the same as 145G51?

Yes. N150 is the Middle East/Africa designation. Same battery.

### Can I use a 145G51 in a passenger car?

No. Too large (508 x 222 mm), too heavy (30+ kg), different terminal size. Passenger car trays cannot accommodate.

### What is the service life?

2-4 years in commercial service. Vibration and deep cycling are primary wear factors. Regular electrolyte level checks extend life (Pb-Sb grids consume some water).

### For 24V truck systems?

Use two 145G51 batteries in series. Ensure both batteries are same age and specification for balanced charging.
