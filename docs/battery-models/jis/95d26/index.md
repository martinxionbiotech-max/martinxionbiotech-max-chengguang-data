## How to identify this battery

The 95D26 uses the JIS D26 case — 256 × 175 × 220 mm — with JIS T1 post terminals and a B00 top-frame hold-down. The label shows "95D26" (or a market alias such as NS95). Confirm the case and terminal before fitment.

Common fitments: larger sedans, SUVs and light commercial vehicles in the Japanese/Asian market. Fitment is a cross-reference decision — always confirm against the vehicle or fleet requirement.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the dimensions of the 95D26?

The 95D26 uses the JIS D26 case, approximately 260 × 173 × 202-225 mm (L × W × H). Confirm the exact case, terminal and hold-down for your application.

### Is the 95D26 interchangeable with a DIN battery?

Not automatically. JIS and DIN batteries differ in case dimensions, hold-down (B00 vs B13) and terminal type, so a near-equivalent Ah rating does not make them drop-in replacements.

### Can the 95D26 be supplied under my brand?

Yes — the 95D26 is available for OEM and private-label programs. Specification, sampling, branding and packaging are confirmed on inquiry.

### What standard is the 95D26?

JIS (JIS D 5301), the Japanese national standard for lead-acid starter batteries.
