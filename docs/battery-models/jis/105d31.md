The 105D31 is the largest JIS D-series battery — a high-capacity, high-CCA model for large SUVs, light commercial vehicles, and demanding diesel applications. It is the direct upgrade from the 65D26 for vehicles needing more starting power.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 105D31 vs 65D26?

105D31 is 46 mm longer with significantly higher capacity (90Ah vs 65Ah) and CCA (650A vs 550A). Only fits vehicles with the longer battery tray.

### What do N70/N80/N90 mean?

N-series names (N70/N80/N90) are regional marketing designations. N70 = 70Ah class, N80 = 80Ah, N90 = 90Ah. All share the same 105D31 case.

### For what diesel engines?

Up to ~4.0L diesel in pickups and SUVs. For larger diesels (trucks/buses), use heavy-duty 145G51 or 190H52.
