## How to Identify This Battery

Distinctive JIS D-series case: **260 mm long x 173 mm wide** with T1 small post terminals. The top label may show any of these designations depending on the target market:

| Label | Region |
|-------|--------|
| **65D26** | Most global markets |
| **NS70** | Southeast Asia, Australia |
| **N50** | Middle East, Africa |
| **N50Z** | High-CCA variant (some markets) |

All refer to the same physical battery. Verify by measuring: 260 mm length, T1 terminals, L- R+ polarity. The 65D26 is often confused with the shorter 55D23 (232 mm) — the 28 mm length difference is the key distinguishing mark.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does 65D26 mean?

The JIS model code breaks down as: **65** = performance class (capacity/CCA tier — higher = more), **D** = width class (D = wide case, approximately 173 mm), **26** = approximate case length in centimeters (26 cm = 260 mm). Higher performance numbers indicate higher capacity and CCA within the same case family.

### Is NS70 the same as 65D26?

**Yes** — they are the same physical battery. 'NS' is the traditional Japanese naming convention used in Southeast Asia, Australia, and New Zealand. A battery labeled 'NS70' from a reputable manufacturer is dimensionally and electrically equivalent to a 65D26. Always verify the CCA and Ah ratings on the label, as regional specifications may differ slightly.

### What is the difference between 65D26 and 55D26?

They share identical case dimensions (260 x 173 mm) and terminals. The difference is electrical:
- **65D26**: ~65Ah, ~550 CCA — standard specification
- **55D26**: ~55Ah, ~450 CCA — economy variant for price-sensitive markets

Both fit the same vehicles. The 55D26 is typically 10-15% lower cost.

### Can a 65D26 be used for diesel engines?

Yes — the 65D26 is commonly used in diesel vehicles up to approximately 3.0L displacement (Toyota Hilux, Fortuner, Isuzu D-Max). For larger diesel engines (>3.5L) or vehicles operated in cold climates, consider upgrading to a 105D31 (longer case, higher CCA) or a heavy-duty model such as 145G51.

### How long does a 65D26 battery last?

- **Temperate climates** (15-25degC average): 3-5 years typical service life
- **Hot climates** (Middle East, Africa, 35-45degC): 18-30 months — heat is the #1 factor accelerating battery aging
- **Factors reducing life**: Frequent short trips (incomplete recharge), aftermarket electrical accessories, corroded terminals, faulty alternator/voltage regulator

Regular charging system checks (alternator output 13.8-14.4V) can significantly extend battery life in hot climates.
