The 95E41 is a high-capacity JIS battery for large SUVs, light trucks, and diesel applications. At 100Ah with 750 CCA, it delivers substantially more power than the standard D-series.

## How to Identify This Battery

Extra-long case (410 mm) — significantly longer than 65D26 (260 mm). T1 small terminals. Label shows '95E41', 'W105', or 'N100'. The 410 mm length is unmistakable.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 95E41 vs 105D31?

95E41 is longer (410 vs 306 mm) but narrower (176 vs 173 mm). Both ~100Ah class. Different vehicle fitment.

### Is N100 the same as 95E41?

N100 is the Middle East/Africa designation for the same battery. Both indicate 100Ah class.

### What diesel vehicles use 95E41?

Large diesel SUVs and light trucks — Land Cruiser, Patrol. Higher CCA (750A) handles diesel cold-start requirements.
