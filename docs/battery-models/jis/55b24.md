## How to Identify This Battery

Narrow case (129 mm wide) with T1 small terminals. Label shows '55B24', 'N45', or 'N40'. The 129 mm width is the key identifier — much narrower than D-series (173 mm).

## Vehicle Applications

Small passenger cars, compact sedans, kei cars. For 1.3L-1.6L gasoline engines in temperate to warm climates.

### Compatible Vehicles

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What vehicles use a 55B24?

Compact Japanese vehicles — Toyota Vios/Yaris, Honda City/Jazz, Suzuki Swift. Verify tray: 238 x 129 mm.

### Is 55B24 the same as N45?

Same physical case. N45 typically indicates 45Ah. Chengguang 55B24 is rated 45Ah covering N40 and N45 designations.

### Can I upgrade to a larger battery?

D-series batteries (55D23, 65D26) are wider (173 mm). Only possible if your tray accommodates the extra 44 mm width.
