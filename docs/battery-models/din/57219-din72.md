## How to Identify This Battery

Standard DIN case with B13 bottom rail and DIN standard post terminals. Label shows '57219' or 'DIN72'. In the DIN battery hierarchy, DIN72 sits between DIN70 (70Ah) and DIN75 (75Ah) — the label Ah rating is the primary differentiator within the mid-range DIN family.
