# 56638 Battery — DIN66, 566.38

The 56638 (DIN66) is the most common entry-level DIN battery — a compact European-spec battery for small-to-mid passenger vehicles. It is the direct DIN counterpart to the JIS 65D26 in terms of market segment.

## How to Identify This Battery

Standard DIN case (278 mm long x 175 mm wide). B13 bottom rail hold-down (lip at base — no top frame). DIN standard post terminals. Label shows '56638' or 'DIN66'.

## Vehicle Applications

## Frequently Asked Questions

### DIN66 vs 65D26?

Different standards. DIN66 uses B13 bottom rail hold-down vs B00 top frame on JIS 65D26. DIN66 is 278 mm long, 65D26 is 260 mm. Not directly interchangeable without adapter.

### What vehicles use DIN66?

Compact European cars — VW Golf, Opel Astra, Peugeot 308, Renault Megane. Common in European, North African, and Middle Eastern markets.
