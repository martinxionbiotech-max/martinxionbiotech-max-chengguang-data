## How to Identify This Battery

Standard DIN case: 278 mm long x 175 mm wide. B13 bottom rail hold-down — a lip at the base of the battery that locks into the vehicle's battery tray. DIN standard post terminals (O19.5mm positive, O17.9mm negative). Label shows '56638', 'DIN66', or '566.38'. Shorter and wider than JIS equivalents.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### DIN66 vs 65D26 — which should I buy?

Depends on your vehicle. European cars use DIN (B13 hold-down, DIN posts). Japanese cars use JIS (B00 clamp, T1 posts). They are NOT directly interchangeable. Check your vehicle manual or measure your battery tray.

### What vehicles use DIN66?

VW Golf, Opel Astra, Peugeot 308, Renault Megane — compact European cars. The most common European battery size.

### Can I use a DIN66 in a Toyota?

Only if your Toyota is a European-market model with a factory DIN battery tray. Most Asian-market Toyotas use JIS batteries (B00 hold-down). Verify your tray type.

### What is the difference between DIN and JIS hold-down?

DIN uses B13 — a lip at the battery base that slides under a rail in the tray. JIS uses B00 — a metal frame that clamps across the battery top. They are completely different mounting systems.
