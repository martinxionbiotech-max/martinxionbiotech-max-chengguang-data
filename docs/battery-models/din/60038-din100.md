## Frequently Asked Questions

### DIN100 vs 95E41?

Both ~100Ah. DIN100 uses B13 bottom rail and is 393 mm long. 95E41 uses B00 top frame and is 410 mm long. Not interchangeable.

### What vehicles need DIN100?

Large European luxury vehicles (Audi A8, BMW 7 Series, Mercedes S-Class) and high-spec diesels. The 870 CCA handles large diesel engines in European climates.

### DIN100 for trucks?

For passenger car batteries — NOT a heavy-duty truck battery. For commercial trucks, use JIS heavy-duty models (145G51, 190H52) or contact Chengguang for DIN commercial vehicle options.
