## Key Concepts

### CCA (Cold Cranking Amps)
The current a battery can deliver at -18degC (0degF) for 30 seconds while maintaining voltage above 7.2V. Higher CCA = better cold-weather starting.

### Ah (Ampere-Hour / C20)
The battery's energy storage capacity. C20 means discharged over 20 hours. A 65Ah battery can deliver 3.25A for 20 hours.

### RC (Reserve Capacity)
Minutes a battery can deliver 25A at 27degC before dropping below 10.5V. Important for vehicles with heavy electrical loads.
