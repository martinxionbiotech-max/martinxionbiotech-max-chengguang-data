# Group 27 — BCI Group Size Reference

BCI **Group 27** is a North American battery group size used for larger passenger cars, SUVs, light trucks and marine/RV. This page is a **group-size reference**: it documents the standard case dimensions and typical 12 V lead-acid ratings for the group, not a specific Chengguang stock model.

!!! info "Standard reference, not a stock SKU"
    BCI group dimensions are published standard data. The capacity and CCA figures below are **typical ranges** for this group and vary by manufacturer and technology (SLI / EFB / AGM). ## How to Identify This Group

Mid-to-large BCI group; higher capacity than Group 24 at the same width. Group 27 is identified by its case footprint (306 × 173 × 225 mm) rather than by a performance grade — the BCI group number carries no capacity information by itself. Check the battery label for the group number, or measure the tray: the length and width must match the group footprint, and the height must clear the hold-down and hood.

## Typical Applications

BCI Group 27 is commonly used for larger passenger cars, SUVs, light trucks and marine/RV. Fitment is a cross-reference decision — always confirm against the vehicle or fleet requirement.

## Regional Markets

North America (core market). Outside North America, vehicles built for BCI fitments (export models, US/Canadian vehicle parcs) may use this group; otherwise the local market standard (JIS or DIN/EN) applies.

## Equivalent & Cross-Reference

DIN/EN H7 / LN4 (315 × 175 × 190 mm) is close in footprint — verify terminal type and hold-down.

!!! warning "Groups are not automatically interchangeable"
    Even where footprints are close, terminal type and hold-down differ between standards. A near-equivalent dimension does **not** mean a drop-in replacement.
