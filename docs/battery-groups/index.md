# BCI Battery Group Sizes

**BCI** (Battery Council International) group numbers define the case footprint and terminal layout of North American automotive batteries. This section documents the common group sizes as **standard references**.

!!! info "Standard reference — not a stock listing"
    The [Battery Models](../battery-models/index.md) section lists Chengguang's **listed JIS and DIN/EN stock models**. This section documents **BCI group sizes** as standard references; these groups are supplied as an **OEM / private-label development on request**.

## Common BCI Groups

| Group | Dimensions (L × W × H) | Typical Capacity | Typical CCA | Terminal | Typical applications |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Group 24](group-24.md) | 260 × 173 × 225 mm | ~70 Ah | ~600 A SAE | SAE top post | Passenger cars, light trucks, marine/RV |
| [Group 27](group-27.md) | 306 × 173 × 225 mm | ~90 Ah | ~800 A SAE | SAE top post | Larger cars, SUVs, light trucks |
| [Group 31](group-31.md) | 330 × 173 × 240 mm | ~100 Ah | ~925 A SAE | Post / stud | Trucks, buses, heavy-duty commercial |
| [4D](4d.md) | 527 × 222 × 251 mm | ~140 Ah | ~1,100 A SAE | Stud / bolt | Heavy trucks, buses, construction |
| [8D](8d.md) | 527 × 283 × 251 mm | ~200 Ah | ~1,400 A SAE | Stud / bolt | Heavy trucks, off-highway, large marine |

!!! warning "Typical values"
    Group dimensions are standardised; capacity and CCA are **typical ranges that vary by manufacturer and technology** (SLI / EFB / AGM). CCA is test-method dependent (SAE at −18 °C). Confirm the datasheet for any specific product.

## BCI Group Numbers vs JIS / DIN Names

BCI group numbers carry **no performance information** — a group number defines only the footprint and terminal layout. This is the opposite of the JIS naming rule, where the numeric prefix is a capacity/performance grade. Two batteries in the same BCI group can differ widely in capacity and CCA.

For the standard families, see the [standards guides](../standards/index.md): [JIS](../standards/jis.md), [DIN](../standards/din.md), [BCI](../standards/bci.md).
