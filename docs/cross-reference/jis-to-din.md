## :material-ruler: Key Differences Between JIS and DIN

| Feature | JIS | DIN |
|---------|-----|-----|
| **Hold-down** | B00 — Top frame clamp | B13 — Bottom rail lip |
| **Terminal** | T1 post (small or large) | Standard post |
| **Case width** | Varies (129, 173, 176, 222, 278 mm) | Standard 175 mm |
| **Case height** | Taller (202-270 mm) | Shorter (175-190 mm) |
| **Common markets** | Asia, Africa, Middle East, Oceania | Europe, North/West Africa, Middle East |

!!! danger "Never Assume Direct Interchangeability"
    JIS and DIN batteries with similar Ah ratings are **NOT** plug-and-play replacements. They differ in: hold-down mechanism (B00 vs B13), case dimensions, terminal post diameter, and sometimes polarity layout. Using a JIS battery in a DIN vehicle (or vice versa) may require an adapter tray and terminal modification. **Always consult your vehicle manual and measure your battery tray.**
